How It Works

Most products try to wipe bacteria out. Staph Aid takes a different approach, from the inside.

1

One capsule, with a meal

17 billion CFU of two spore-forming strains plus Vitamin C, in a single daily capsule.

2

Built to survive the trip

Spore-forming probiotics are hardy enough to make it through your stomach to your gut. [S]

3

Interrupts, doesn’t kill

B. subtilis produces fengycins, which appear to interrupt the signalling Staph uses to colonize, rather than killing it outright. LEGAL
