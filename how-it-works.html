Science & Certifications
The research
Staph Aid was formulated around published research on spore probiotics and staph. In a phase 2, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial funded in part by the US National Institutes of Health, 115 adults in Thailand who carried staph took a Bacillus subtilis probiotic or a placebo once a day for 30 days. The probiotic group had far less staph in the gut and nose afterward; the placebo group did not.¹ The trial measured colonization, not infections.
