2. Decisions only you can make

Money and offers

  1. Prices. The site uses $24.99 / $49.98 / $74.97 on Refills and $29.99 / $59.98 / $89.97 one-time, copied from the live store. Confirm, because the live site also shows a second, older price list.
  2. One discount number. The popup says 60% off, the site says up to 70% off, and the live site says "Save 25%" on Refills when the real saving is 16%. Pick the numbers and they go everywhere.
  3. The $70 Kit value. We need the basis for $70 (the retail price of each item). Advertised "value" figures get challenged.
  4. Shipping. Your policies state free shipping and delivery times differently in different places. What is the real rule?

Legal and policies

  1. Refunds vs. the guarantee. The refund policy says no returns on sale items, but everything is on sale and every page promises a 30-day money-back guarantee. One of them has to change.
  2. Text messages. The site collects phone numbers for marketing texts, but the privacy policy has no SMS section. This must be added before launch.
  3. Governing law. The terms do not name a state. Your lawyer should fill it in.
  4. Lines marked LEGAL. Some copy restored from the live site says the product helps with staph or infections. Those lines need your lawyer to approve them or they come off.
  5. The "Her Story" video. It includes prevention claims and a wound image. Keep, trim, or drop?
  6. "100% Organic" on Billy's badge. Only USDA-certified products can say organic. Do you have the certificate? If not, the badge text changes.

Proof we need before a number goes up

  1. Review and order counts. The site now shows the live site's figures: 15,732 reviews and 100,000+ orders delivered. The live site also has an image that says "5,000+ 5 star reviews", which doesn't match. Confirm both numbers from the review app and your order history.
  2. Survey numbers. 93.6% / 88.3% / 88.2% stay off until you send where they come from.
  3. Testing panel. The live site shows the same four testing numbers Grüns uses. We need your real lab report (COA), or the panel stays as XX.
  4. 17 billion CFU. This needs the lab report or label that backs it.
  5. Ingredient lists. The "Free From" list and the "5 Reasons" card don't list the same things. Send the correct allergen list.
  6. Reviews. Which reviews app (Judge.me or Junip), and can your existing reviews be imported? Stars stay hidden until there are real ones.

Things only you can send

  1. Your founder story for the Our Story page.
  2. Real photos of customers (with written permission) and the product. The people in the current photos are AI-generated placeholders, and so is Billy.
  3. Old links. Which of the six live product URLs should redirect where.

Already settled, so no need to revisit: the Grüns-style look (you approved it), and keeping your live copy word for word (anything risky is flagged, not rewritten). Send notes page by page. Screenshots with a circle on them are perfect.
