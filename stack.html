What the new site runs on, and what it costs

The short version: stay on Shopify, put a custom theme on it, and add four low-cost apps. That is about $84 a month at launch and grows with sales. Grüns uses premium apps: their subscription app alone would cost about $750 a month at our likely early volume. We don't need that until sales justify it, and every app below can be swapped later without rebuilding the site.

Card fees are separate and usually the biggest cost: Shopify Payments takes 2.9% + 30¢ per order on Basic (about $365 on $10k of sales at a $40 average order). Every Shopify store pays this, whatever the apps.

4. What we are not recommending

  1. A page-builder app (Replo, GemPages, PageFly: $30–$300/mo). The theme's own sections let you clone an offer page without code, which is the job these apps do.
  2. Going "headless" (a custom front end talking to Shopify). It's faster in theory, but it costs far more to build and maintain, and gains nothing at this size.
  3. A custom Stripe checkout instead of Shopify. You'd lose Shop Pay, which returning buyers use for one-tap checkout, and we'd have to build tax, subscriptions and order management ourselves.
  4. A loyalty app right now. It's worth adding once there are enough repeat buyers to reward.

5. Questions for you before we build

  1. Which subscription app runs the live store today, and how many active subscribers are on it? The live store already sells subscriptions through an app we couldn't identify from the outside. Moving live subscribers without double-charging or dropping anyone is the riskiest step of the switch, and the plan depends on this answer.
  2. Which Shopify plan are you on now? If it's already Basic, the platform cost doesn't change.
  3. Do you already pay for an email or SMS tool? We saw no email, SMS or reviews app on the live storefront.
  4. Who ships the orders (you, a warehouse, a 3PL)? That decides whether a shipping app is needed.

Prices were checked on each vendor's pricing page or Shopify App Store listing on September 25, 2026, and they change. The Klaviyo figure at 2,500 contacts is an estimate between published tiers. Theme build, photography and illustration are one-off costs and aren't included here.
