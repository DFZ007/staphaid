Science & Certifications
The research
Staph Aid was formulated around published research on spore probiotics and staph. In a phase 2, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial funded in part by the US National Institutes of Health, 115 adults in Thailand who carried staph took a Bacillus subtilis probiotic or a placebo once a day for 30 days. The probiotic group had far less staph in the gut and nose afterward; the placebo group did not.¹ The trial measured colonization, not infections. LEGAL The trial used its own strain (MB40); Staph Aid uses a different B. subtilis strain and has not been tested in a trial of its own.
¹Piewngam P. et al., The Lancet Microbe, 2023.
